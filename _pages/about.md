<section class="profile-section" id="research" aria-labelledby="research-title">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <p class="eyebrow">Research</p>
    <h2 id="research-title">Methods that make advanced models more useful</h2>
  </div>

  <div class="focus-grid">
    <article class="focus-card">
      <span class="focus-number">01</span>
      <h3>Efficient LLM adaptation</h3>
      <p>GASDU uses dynamic sparse updates to fine-tune large language models without adding inference latency, reaching up to <strong>10.6× faster training</strong> with <strong>3.3× lower peak memory</strong>.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="focus-card">
      <span class="focus-number">02</span>
      <h3>Knowledge distillation &amp; MoE</h3>
      <p>I develop compact teacher-student systems and mixture-of-experts architectures that preserve reliability while reducing the storage and compute required for deployment.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="focus-card">
      <span class="focus-number">03</span>
      <h3>Functional data &amp; scientific ML</h3>
      <p>Our SpectraFRM framework standardizes SERS measurements across instruments, enabling at least <strong>94% post-transformation identification accuracy</strong> for 15 of 20 analytes.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="focus-card">
      <span class="focus-number">04</span>
      <h3>Forecasting for public health</h3>
      <p>My work combines internet search behavior and autoregressive signals to improve COVID-19 hospitalization forecasts at both national and state levels.</p>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>
